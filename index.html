Questions

Before you send the brief.

Four things people ask before the first message. Anything else, send it with the brief.

Do you work in Arabic?

Yes — Arabic-first or bilingual layouts. RTL is designed properly, not mirrored as an afterthought. Frosty and Labelle are both Arabic.

What if I don't have copy or photos?

Normal. I write the page structure and can draft copy. Photography can come later — a real phone photo beats a stock image.

Can we do it cheaper?

Sometimes, by cutting scope — fewer pages, no booking system. I'd rather remove work than discount the same work.

Do you only build on WordPress?

WordPress, WooCommerce and Odoo are the core. Shopify when the store already lives there, as Labelle does.

Tell me what the site needs to do.

Two minutes on the form, or one word in a DM. You get three specific fixes within 24 hours, free.
