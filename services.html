FAQ

The questions I get before every project.

Something else? Ask directly — DM “SITE”.

How fast can you start? +

I take one build a week. Usually there's a slot within seven days, and a WordPress site is live three to seven days after that.

Do I own the website, hosting and domain? +

Yes. Domain, hosting and WordPress admin are in your name from day one. On final payment you own the design and code, and you get a walkthrough video so you're not dependent on me.

Do you guarantee more sales? +

No honest person can. What I guarantee: a measured baseline, a site built around one action, and 30 days after launch to fix anything that isn't working as agreed.

What are the payment terms? +

50% to start, 50% on launch. Egypt: InstaPay or bank transfer. International: Wise or bank transfer in USD. No work starts before the deposit clears.

Can you fix my existing site instead? +

Often, yes. The free audit tells you which is cheaper. If a targeted fix gets you 80% of the result, I'll say so and charge for the fix — not for a rebuild.
