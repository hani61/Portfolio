01 · Marketing agency · Cairo

An agency site that has to earn the meeting.

WordPress Lead generation Copy structure

Timeline

6 days, one revision round

The one action

Book a 15-minute call

Layout preview. Real screenshots and measured results go here as each build ships.

What was wrong

A nine-page brochure with three competing calls to action on the homepage and a contact form that emailed an address nobody checked. Their best proof — two named results with real numbers — was buried on page six.

What I changed

  • ✓Four pages instead of nine. Every page asks for the call, once.
  • ✓Proof moved above the fold: one client, one number, one sentence.
  • ✓Enquiries land in email and WhatsApp, with a reply-time promise on the button.
  • ✓Services rewritten as outcomes the client can price, not a list of disciplines.
  • ✓Under 2s load on mobile, since their buyers open links from WhatsApp.

02 · Dental clinic · Cairo

A clinic that lives or dies on the booking button.

WordPress Booking flow Arabic + English

Timeline

8 days, two revision rounds

The one action

Book an appointment

Layout preview. Real screenshots and measured results go here as each build ships.

What was wrong

The phone number was an image, the booking link was on the contact page, and the treatments were described in language patients don't use. Most visitors were on a phone, at night, deciding whether to call tomorrow.

What I changed

  • ✓Booking bar pinned to the top and a sticky mobile button — always one tap away.
  • ✓Tap-to-call and WhatsApp as equal options, because half the patients won't fill a form.
  • ✓Treatments explained in plain language with price ranges and visit length.
  • ✓Doctor credentials and clinic photos near the top: trust before treatment.
  • ✓Directions, parking and opening hours in the footer of every page.

03 · Ice cream shop · New Damietta

One page that answers: what, open now, how do I order.

Arabic RTL Menu + prices Live open/closed

The shop

فروستي آيس كريم · 4.2 of 5 from 483 Google reviews

The one action

Order or call, one tap away

Open the live page ↗

Live build. Gallery photos are drawn placeholders until the shop supplies originals.

What it needed

The shop lived on a Google Maps pin and a Facebook page. Late-night customers had to piece together the menu, the prices and whether it was still open — usually on a phone, at midnight.

What I built

  • ✓A single Arabic (RTL) page, written in Egyptian Arabic and tuned for one hand at night.
  • ✓Live open/closed status computed from the schedule in Africa/Cairo — including the after-midnight window, so “closed” is never wrong.
  • ✓All 21 menu items in five groups with prices (45–135 EGP), taken from the official delivery menu.
  • ✓A cone builder: pick 2–6 scoops, watch the cone redraw and the price update.
  • ✓Phone, WhatsApp and directions as real tap targets, plus IceCreamShop structured data and a layout that holds from 320px up.

Why it's here

A shop with no website now has one link that answers everything a customer asks before walking in.

05 · Specialty coffee · Maadi, Cairo

A coffee counter that has to answer one question: are you open right now?

Live hours Full priced menu Directions

The shop

stay. · Victoria Villa, Maadi as Sarayat Al Gharbeyah · 4.9 of 5 from 60 Google reviews

The one action

Get directions, walk in

Open the live site ↗

Live build. The production domain is still to come — the shop's Google Maps listing is linked throughout.

What it needed

A 4.9 rating from 60 reviews, and nothing to show for it but a pin on Google Maps. The shop sits inside the courtyard of Victoria Villa, easy to walk past — so anyone deciding at 11pm needed to see it was open until one, what it pours, and what that costs.

What I built

  • ✓An open-now clock in Maadi time, computed from the real schedule — including Thursday's earlier 12:30 close.
  • ✓The whole menu with prices in Egyptian pounds: black coffee, matcha, milk drinks, iced, non-coffee, add-ons and the four cold foams.
  • ✓The 4.9 rating and three reviews quoted word for word, credited to the people who wrote them.
  • ✓Hours, address, Plus code and one-tap Google Maps directions, so "where is it" never needs a message.
  • ✓A gallery of the shopfront, pergola and cups, plus Instagram and the “for another cup” line carried from hero to footer.

Why it's here

A neighbourhood spot with a near-perfect rating now has a page that looks like the room — and tells you it's open before you walk over.

The pattern

Different industries, identical diagnosis.

None of these needed a redesign. Each one needed a decision: what is this page asking the visitor to do? Once that's answered, the design writes itself and the price becomes easy to justify.

  • One action per page

    If everything is a call to action, nothing is. Every extra button splits attention.

  • Proof before promise

    One named result beats a wall of adjectives. If there's no result yet, show the work itself.

  • The phone is the website

    Built and tested on a mid-range Android on 4G. That's where the customer actually is.
