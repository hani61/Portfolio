01 · Marketing agency · Cairo

An agency site that has to earn the meeting.

WordPress · Lead generation · Copy structure · 6 days, one revision round

A nine-page brochure with three competing calls to action on the homepage and a contact form that emailed an address nobody checked. Their best proof — two named results with real numbers — was buried on page six.

What I changed

  • Four pages instead of nine, each asking for the call once
  • Proof moved above the fold: one client, one number, one sentence
  • Enquiries land in email and WhatsApp, with a reply-time promise on the button
  • Services rewritten as outcomes the client can price, not a list of disciplines
  • Under 2s load on mobile, since their buyers open links from WhatsApp

The one action: book a 15-minute call. Baseline captured on launch day; the 30-day enquiry numbers get published here, good or bad.

02 · Dental clinic · Cairo

A clinic that lives or dies on the booking button.

WordPress · Booking flow · Arabic and English · 8 days, two revision rounds

The phone number was an image, the booking link was on the contact page, and treatments were described in language patients don't use. Most visitors were on a phone, at night, deciding whether to call tomorrow.

What I changed

  • Booking bar pinned to the top and a sticky mobile button, always one tap away
  • Tap-to-call and WhatsApp as equal options, because half the patients won't fill a form
  • Treatments explained in plain language with price ranges and visit length
  • Credentials and clinic photos near the top: trust before treatment
  • Directions, parking and opening hours in the footer of every page

The one action: book an appointment. Baseline captured on launch day; the 30-day booking numbers get published here too.

03 · Ice cream shop · New Damietta

One page that answers: what, open now, how do I order.

Arabic RTL · Menu and prices · Live open status · فروستي آيس كريم · 4.2 of 5 from 483 Google reviews

The shop lived on a Google Maps pin and a Facebook page. Late-night customers had to piece together the menu, the prices and whether it was still open — usually on a phone, at midnight.

What I built

  • A single Arabic (RTL) page, written in Egyptian Arabic for one hand at night
  • Live open and closed status from the Africa/Cairo schedule, including after midnight
  • All 21 menu items in five groups with prices (45–135 EGP)
  • A cone builder: 2–6 scoops, the cone redraws, the price updates
  • Phone, WhatsApp and directions as tap targets, with structured data and a 320px floor

04 · Beauty and cosmetics · Damietta

A storefront judged on whether browsing feels effortless.

Shopify · E-commerce · English and Arabic · Moria Mall, New Damietta

Beauty shoppers arrive knowing the category, not the brand: skincare, makeup, fragrance or haircare. The store had to get them to the right shelf in two taps, in their own language, on a phone.

What I built

  • A Shopify storefront where the product photography does the selling
  • Categories and collections, one obvious route into each product line
  • New arrivals and best sellers kept current, offers kept out of the catalogue
  • Short cart and checkout — variant, quantity, delivery — plus search by name
  • Newsletter signup and English and Arabic on the same store

05 · Specialty coffee · Maadi, Cairo

A coffee counter that has to answer: are you open right now?

Live hours · Full priced menu · Directions · stay. Victoria Villa, Maadi as Sarayat Al Gharbeyah · 4.9 of 5 from 60 Google reviews

A 4.9 rating from 60 reviews and nothing to show for it but a pin on Google Maps. The shop sits inside the courtyard of Victoria Villa, easy to walk past — so anyone deciding at 11pm needed to see it was open until one, what it pours and what that costs.

What I built

  • An open-now clock in Maadi time, including Thursday's earlier 12:30 close
  • The whole menu with prices in EGP, from black coffee to the four cold foams
  • The 4.9 rating and three reviews quoted verbatim, credited to their writers
  • Hours, address, Plus code and one-tap Google Maps directions
  • A gallery of the shopfront, pergola and cups, plus Instagram
